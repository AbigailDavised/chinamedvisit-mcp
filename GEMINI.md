# ChinaMedVisit Gemini CLI Extension

Connects Gemini CLI to **ChinaMedVisit** (`https://chinamedvisit.com/api/mcp`), providing stateless MCP tools for:
- Procedure cost estimations for China Top Tier-3A International Medical Centers (PUMCH, Fuwai, Tiantan, Ruijin)
- Self-pay procedure price benchmarks vs US / UK private hospital rates (PET-CT, 3T MRI, painless endoscopy)
- AI clinical medical travel itinerary planning and chief physician triage
- China medical tourism visa (S2, L, 144-hour visa-free transit) compliance

Website: https://chinamedvisit.com
