## 🔧 Available MCP Tools

### `estimate_china_medical_cost`
Compare out-of-pocket costs in Chinese RMB and USD between China Tier-3A International Medical Centers (IMC), Private Clinics, US averages, and UK private rates across PET-CT, 3T MRI, endoscopy, and surgical care.

### `lookup_tier3a_international_hospital`
Look up verified Tier-3A (三甲) public hospital International Medical Centers (IMC) in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Chengdu with official addresses, phones, registration portals, and card acceptance.

### `get_medical_tourism_itinerary_plan`
Generate a day-by-day China Tier-3A hospital diagnostic schedule (fasting bloodwork, 3T MRI/PET-CT same-day imaging turnaround, and chief physician MDT review) with mandatory medical dossier requirements.

### `get_bilingual_hospital_phrasebook`
Retrieve essential English-Chinese-Pinyin hospital communication phrases for registration desks, symptom description, drug allergy alerts, radiology consent, and pharmacy pickups.

### `check_china_medical_visa_and_payment_prep`
Verify payment methods accepted by Chinese public Tier-3A IMC desks (Visa, Mastercard, Alipay/WeChat Pay foreign card binding, CNY cash) and passport registration rules for 240h TWOV vs medical visas.

### `compare_tier3a_imc_vs_private_hospital`
Compare China national academic public Tier-3A International Medical Centers (PUMCH, Huashan, Ruijin) against private international clinics across academic subspecialty depth, cost, wait time, and English support.
